### FR-10 Observation, behavior characterization and research

@FR-10 | Observation and analytics
P: —
S: The Gateway SHALL capture policy-selected observation parameters and meta-metrics, learn statistical baselines of normal behavior, classify activity by standard-deviation distance from those baselines, and export research datasets.
A: E-29; E-30; E-31; E-32
V: Analysis | Review the verification results of every FR-10 child requirement; confirm each has passed.
SRC: ARCH §2.1 items 6–9; ARCH §9; ARCH §10
R: The Gateway is a vehicle for developing run-time policy and trend-analysis techniques.

@FR-10.1 | Observable emission
P: FR-10
S: Each instrumented element (E-01, E-02, E-04, E-06, E-08, E-09, E-10, E-11, E-12, E-15, E-17, E-18) SHALL emit its catalog-defined raw observables keyed by correlation ID to the Observation Collector (E-29) without blocking its processing path.
A: E-29; F-80; I-29
V: Test | Stall E-29 consumption; confirm that instrumented elements continue processing and that drops are counted.
SRC: ARCH §4 E-29; ARCH §6.2 I-29
R: Observation must not change enforcement timing.

@FR-10.2 | Timing spans
P: FR-10
S: Each instrumented element SHALL emit a timing span per stage containing stage ID, monotonic start and end, CPU time, correlation ID and the ID of the policy object that caused the work.
A: E-29; F-83; I-29; X-10
V: Test | Trace a call; confirm a span for every FR-4.3 stage with each field.
SRC: ARCH §10.4
R: Enables per-policy time-cost attribution.

@FR-10.3 | Record assembly
P: FR-10
S: The Observation Collector (E-29) SHALL assemble one observation record per call from the observables sharing its correlation ID, after the call's release or failure.
A: E-29; F-80; F-81
V: Test | Make 1,000 calls; confirm 1,000 records, each joined correctly.
SRC: ARCH §4 E-29
R: Per-call records are the analysis unit.

@FR-10.4 | Parameter selection
P: FR-10
S: The Observation Collector (E-29) SHALL include in each record only the parameters named by the effective Observation Policy or Research Campaign plus the identifiers P-001, P-004, P-022, P-023, P-080 and P-087.
A: E-29; F-82
V: Test | Publish a policy selecting 5 parameters; confirm records contain exactly those plus the identifiers.
SRC: ARCH §4 E-29; ARCH §10.3
R: Capture follows policy.

@FR-10.5 | Feature extraction
P: FR-10
S: The Observation Collector (E-29) SHALL compute each derived parameter according to its Parameter Catalog definition.
A: E-29; F-81; D-11
V: Test | Compare computed features for a fixture call set with reference values; confirm exact match for integer features and agreement within 1e-9 relative error for real-valued features.
SRC: ARCH §10.3
R: Feature correctness underlies the research.

@FR-10.6 | Deterministic sampling
P: FR-10
S: The Observation Collector (E-29) SHALL select normal-band calls for capture by comparing a hash of the correlation ID with the policy sampling rate.
A: E-29; F-82
V: Test | Replay a fixed call set twice at 10%; confirm the same calls are selected and the fraction is 10% ± 1% over 10,000 calls.
SRC: ARCH §9.5 (Sampling)
R: Reproducible sampling.

@FR-10.7 | Capture escalation
P: FR-10
S: When a call's anomaly band is `elevated` or worse, the Observation Collector (E-29) SHALL capture that call in full with the escalation parameters of its band.
A: E-29; F-82; I-31
V: Test | Trigger an abnormal fixture during 10% sampling; confirm full capture with escalation parameters.
SRC: ARCH §9.5 (Capture escalation)
R: Abnormal behavior is always recorded.

@FR-10.8 | Drop accounting
P: FR-10
S: The Observation Collector (E-29) SHALL count every observable or record dropped or sampled down, by reason, and SHALL report the counts as parameter P-246.
A: E-29; F-82; F-83
V: Test | Induce backpressure; confirm the counts match the injected drop volume.
SRC: ARCH §4 E-29
R: Silent loss would bias research.

@FR-10.9 | System meta-metrics
P: FR-10
S: The Observation Collector (E-29) SHALL sample the system meta-metrics P-240 to P-250 every 10 seconds.
A: E-29; F-83; D-13
V: Test | Run for 5 minutes; confirm 30 samples per metric.
SRC: ARCH §10.3 (system meta-metrics)
R: Throughput and resource trends.

@FR-10.10 | Gateway-added overhead
P: FR-10
S: The Observation Collector (E-29) SHALL compute parameter P-213 for each call as end-to-end latency minus server execution time minus approval wait time.
A: E-29; F-83
V: Test | Compare P-213 with an independent harness-side measurement for fixture calls; confirm agreement within 1 ms.
SRC: ARCH §10.3 P-213
R: The owner's cost-in-time metric.

@FR-10.11 | Policy cost attribution
P: FR-10
S: The Observation Collector (E-29) SHALL aggregate span time by policy revision, rule ID, filter ID and Observation Policy ID.
A: E-29; F-83; D-13
V: Test | Run fixture traffic with a deliberately slow filter; confirm that filter ranks first in aggregated cost.
SRC: ARCH §10.4
R: Identifies which policy elements cost the most time.

@FR-10.12 | Context key computation
P: FR-10
S: The Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL group observation records by the baseline context dimensions selected in the Observation Policy.
A: E-30; F-84; I-34
V: Test | Configure (principal, tool) contexts; confirm one context per observed pair.
SRC: ARCH §9.3
R: Baselines are per context.

@FR-10.13 | Streaming statistics
P: FR-10
S: The Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL maintain per context and feature the count, mean and M2 accumulator using Welford's online algorithm, with optional exponential decay at the policy half-life.
A: E-30; F-84; D-10
V: Test | Feed a 1,000,000-value fixture series; confirm mean and variance agree with two-pass computation within 1e-9 relative error.
SRC: ARCH §9.2
R: Numerically stable streaming mean and standard deviation.

@FR-10.14 | Feature transforms
P: FR-10
S: The Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL apply the per-feature transform selected in the Observation Policy, from `none`, `log1p` and `box-cox(λ)`, before updating statistics.
A: E-30; F-84
V: Test | Configure each transform; confirm the stored statistics are of transformed values.
SRC: ARCH §9.2 (Skewed features)
R: Sizes and latencies are right-skewed.

@FR-10.15 | Robust statistics option
P: FR-10
S: When robust scoring is selected for a feature, the Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL maintain a t-digest sketch from which median and MAD are derived.
A: E-30; F-84; D-10
V: Test | Compare sketch median and MAD with exact values over a fixture; confirm agreement within 1%.
SRC: ARCH §9.2 (Robust option)
R: Heavy-tailed features.

@FR-10.16 | Categorical and sequence features
P: FR-10
S: The Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL maintain category and tool-transition frequencies per context and SHALL baseline the surprise value −log p of each observed category or transition.
A: E-30; F-84; D-10
V: Test | Feed a fixed transition sequence; confirm the stored frequencies and surprise statistics.
SRC: ARCH §9.2; ARCH §13.1 NEXT_TOOL
R: Detects unusual tool sequences.

@FR-10.17 | Learning exclusions
P: FR-10
S: The Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL exclude from learning the records of denied calls, records classified `abnormal` or `critical`, records from flagged sessions and records outside the learning window.
A: E-30; F-84
V: Test | Feed a mix of excluded and included records; confirm only the included records change the statistics.
SRC: ARCH §9.4 step 1; ARCH §2.2 A-6
R: Defends against baseline poisoning.

@FR-10.18 | Snapshot build
P: FR-10
S: The Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL build an immutable baseline snapshot, containing snapshot ID, digest, contexts, per-feature statistics, sample counts and learning window, at the policy interval or record count and on operator command.
A: E-30; F-85; D-10
V: Test | Trigger a snapshot by each method; inspect the contents and confirm immutability.
SRC: ARCH §9.4 step 2
R: Scoring uses fixed, versioned baselines.

@FR-10.19 | Drift report
P: FR-10
S: The Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL report, for a candidate snapshot against the promoted snapshot, the per-context mean shift in promoted-σ units, the σ ratio and the list of newly characterized contexts.
A: E-30; F-87; I-06
V: Test | Build a candidate from shifted fixture data; confirm the reported shift values.
SRC: ARCH §9.4 step 3
R: Reviewers see what promotion changes.

@FR-10.20 | Promotion
P: FR-10
S: The Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL promote a candidate snapshot on authorized operator approval, or automatically when every drift value is within the policy's automatic-promotion limits.
A: E-30; F-86; I-39
V: Test | Promote manually; then configure limits and confirm automatic promotion within limits and none beyond them.
SRC: ARCH §9.4 step 4; ARCH §19 OD-11
R: Controlled change of the enforcement baseline.

@FR-10.21 | Rollback
P: FR-10
S: The Behavior Baseline Engine (E-30) SHALL re-promote any earlier snapshot on authorized operator command.
A: E-30; F-86; I-39
V: Test | Roll back to a prior snapshot; confirm subsequent scores record its ID.
SRC: ARCH §9.4 step 5
R: Recover from a bad baseline.

@FR-10.22 | Snapshot loading
P: FR-10
S: The Anomaly Scorer (E-31) SHALL load a promoted snapshot into memory, verify its digest and atomically replace the previous snapshot.
A: E-31; F-88; I-39
V: Test | Promote during load; confirm no score uses a partially loaded snapshot and that a corrupted snapshot is rejected.
SRC: ARCH §4 E-31
R: Consistent scoring.

@FR-10.23 | z-score computation
P: FR-10
S: The Anomaly Scorer (E-31) SHALL compute each selected feature's score as z = (x − μ) / max(σ, σ_floor) using the snapshot statistics of the session's context and the same transform used in learning.
A: E-31; F-88; F-89
V: Test | Score fixture values against a known snapshot; confirm z-scores agree with reference computation within 1e-9.
SRC: ARCH §9.2 (Distance)
R: The owner's standard-deviation distance measure.

@FR-10.24 | Robust score
P: FR-10
S: When robust scoring is selected for a feature, the Anomaly Scorer (E-31) SHALL compute z_robust = 0.6745 (x − median) / max(MAD, σ_floor).
A: E-31; F-88; F-89
V: Test | Score fixture values; confirm agreement with reference values.
SRC: ARCH §9.2 (Robust option)
R: Alternative distance for heavy tails.

@FR-10.25 | Score combination
P: FR-10
S: The Anomaly Scorer (E-31) SHALL combine feature scores by maximum absolute z and SHALL record the contributing feature.
A: E-31; F-90
V: Test | Score a fixture with several elevated features; confirm the maximum and the named feature.
SRC: ARCH §9.2 (Multi-feature); ARCH §19 OD-12
R: Explainable combination.

@FR-10.26 | Band classification
P: FR-10
S: The Anomaly Scorer (E-31) SHALL classify the combined score into `normal`, `elevated`, `abnormal` or `critical` using the Observation Policy thresholds, with CP-30 as defaults.
A: E-31; F-90
V: Test | Score values at each boundary; confirm the band.
SRC: ARCH §9.2 (Default sigma bands)
R: Bands drive actions and capture.

@FR-10.27 | Uncharacterized context
P: FR-10
S: When a session's context has fewer than n_min samples in the promoted snapshot, the Anomaly Scorer (E-31) SHALL return the band `uncharacterized`.
A: E-31; F-90
V: Test | Score a new context; confirm `uncharacterized` until n_min is reached in a promoted snapshot.
SRC: ARCH §9.2 (Cold start)
R: No scoring without sufficient data.

@FR-10.28 | Asynchronous scoring
P: FR-10
S: When the Observation Policy selects asynchronous scoring, the Anomaly Scorer (E-31) SHALL score the assembled observation record after release and SHALL apply only alert and capture-escalation actions.
A: E-31; F-90; I-31
V: Test | Configure asynchronous scoring; trigger an abnormal call; confirm an alert, unchanged release timing and no blocking action.
SRC: ARCH §9.5 (Scoring mode); ARCH §19 OD-10
R: Zero hot-path cost option.

@FR-10.29 | Scoring record
P: FR-10
S: The Anomaly Scorer (E-31) SHALL record for each score the snapshot ID, context key, per-feature values and z-scores, combined score, band and resulting actions.
A: E-31; F-90; I-29; I-18
V: Inspection | Inspect the scoring record of an abnormal call.
SRC: ARCH §9.4 step 6
R: Reproducible classification.

@FR-10.30 | Campaign record
P: FR-10
S: The Research Dataset Service (E-32) SHALL manage each research campaign with scope, parameter set, sampling, start, end, storage quota, export grant and state (`draft`, `submitted`, `approved`, `active`, `completed`, `exported`, `expired`).
A: E-32; F-91; D-01
V: Test | Move a campaign through every state; confirm transitions and recorded fields.
SRC: ARCH §7.2 Research Campaign; ARCH §18.5
R: Bounded, governed research capture.

@FR-10.31 | Campaign activation
P: FR-10
S: The Research Dataset Service (E-32) SHALL activate an approved campaign at its start time and deactivate it at its end time or when its storage quota is reached.
A: E-32; F-91
V: Test | Run a campaign with a small quota; confirm deactivation at the quota.
SRC: ARCH §18.5 step 3
R: Time-boxed and bounded capture.

@FR-10.32 | Dataset extraction
P: FR-10
S: The Research Dataset Service (E-32) SHALL extract a dataset containing only records within the campaign's scope and time window.
A: E-32; F-92; I-35
V: Test | Extract a dataset with out-of-scope traffic present; confirm exclusion.
SRC: ARCH §4 E-32
R: Datasets reflect the approved campaign.

@FR-10.33 | Pseudonymization
P: FR-10
S: The Research Dataset Service (E-32) SHALL replace principal, session, end-user and S2 identifiers with HMAC-SHA-256 pseudonyms under a per-dataset key from the Key & Trust Anchor Manager (E-22).

V: Test | Extract two datasets; confirm consistent pseudonyms within each dataset and different pseudonyms across them.
SRC: ARCH §4 E-32; CP-40
R: Research data protection.

@FR-10.34 | Export formats
P: FR-10
S: The Research Dataset Service (E-32) SHALL export datasets in Parquet, CSV and JSON Lines, and the behavior-model and audit subgraphs in GraphML.
A: E-32; F-93; X-11

SRC: ARCH §4 E-32; ARCH §6.1 X-11

@FR-10.35 | Dataset manifest

@FR-10.36 | Export destination
